Sustainable Urban Development: A Case Study of Lucknow 3rd Rank

Author · Solo competitor, BBAU Research Hackathon

  • Built a Python pipeline with OSMnx, Pandas and NumPy to extract and clean large spatial network datasets from OpenStreetMap.
  • Calculated six core network metrics, such as street density and dead-end ratios, to quantify differences between urban zones.
  • Found a spatial data anomaly where a historic monument’s grounds artificially masked a severe local green space deficit.
  • Wrote automated Matplotlib scripts to map street graphs and proposed 14 data-driven infrastructure upgrades.

Dark LLM Triad: AI & Confidentiality

Author
  • Developed the “Dark LLM Triad” framework covering three major risks when governments adopt AI: data leaks (Data Hemorrhage), blind trust in AI output (Overconfidence), and language cost disparities (Script Bias).
  • Created custom Python scoring indexes (DLSI and HRI) that turn high-profile real-world failures into clear, numeric risk metrics.
  • Built a tokenization benchmarking script with Tiktoken and Transformers, showing that Hindi faces a 3.04x cost penalty while Chinese is more cost-effective than English.
